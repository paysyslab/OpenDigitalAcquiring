# Card Acquiring — Introduction

The Merchant App is offered to merchants as a channel for both account holders and non-account holders. Merchants who already hold a bank account can register on the application and link their existing account to receive customer payments. Merchants who do not have an account can also register on the application and request account creation by fulfilling the mandatory requirements for digital account opening; once created, they can link the account and start receiving customer payments.

## NTB Onboarding Options

For a fresh NTB (New-to-Bank) merchant onboarding journey, the merchant is presented with two onboarding options within the application:

1. **Raast Payments**
2. **SoftPOS / mPOS Payments** (based on whether the merchant's mobile device supports NFC)

- If the merchant selects **Raast Payments**, the merchant is onboarded for Raast only.
- If the merchant selects **SoftPOS** or **mPOS**, the merchant is onboarded for the selected SoftPOS/mPOS solution, and Raast is provisioned by default as part of the onboarding.

Merchants can onboard themselves for Raast Payments and SoftPOS/mPOS Payments through the application, with separate and distinct KYC requirements applicable for each onboarding type.

Once a merchant is onboarded for either Raast or SoftPOS/mPOS, the POS onboarding option becomes available on the merchant dashboard after login, allowing the merchant to initiate the alternate onboarding journey if required.

:::note
For SoftPOS/mPOS and POS onboarding, only individual merchant onboarding is supported. No other legal entity types are applicable or required for SoftPOS or mPOS onboarding.
:::
